Wield Beta 16

Version 1.0 (120)

  • Window tiling verifies moves and sizes before treating them as complete. Moving a window between displays retries a missed resize instead of learning the old size as a minimum.
  • Windows in hidden Wield spaces park beyond an outside display edge. Directional moves land on the entry side of the next display, and equally close neighbours follow recent focus.
  • The Caps Lock layer keeps your tap choice when keyboard capture is unavailable and recovers lost holds and mappings after keyboard or session changes.
  • Calculator keeps its input and answer drawer in place while results change. Long answers scroll inside the drawer.
  • Feedback now sends only your message and Bug, Suggestion or Other category. Image attachments and the app-details option have been removed.

Beta 14 and 15 can update through Wield’s updater. Beta 13 and earlier: quit Wield and replace WieldMac.app with this download; keep saved data.

Universal build for macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel. Runtime verification is on macOS 27 / Apple Silicon. Physical multi-display moves, display changes, Caps Lock and keyboard reconnects, sleep and lid events, older macOS, physical Intel, and a full visible walkthrough still need beta testing. Automatic tiling remains off by default.
