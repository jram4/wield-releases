Wield Beta 13

Version 1.0 (115)

  • Replay onboarding anytime from Studio → Activation → Replay onboarding. The small text button sits beside Learn Wield, below the preferences.
  • Replay always starts at Welcome and plays the opening again, even when an earlier setup was left unfinished. Existing commands, settings, practice-app choices, and macOS permission grants are kept.
  • Normal reopening still resumes unfinished setup. Updating or reinstalling Wield keeps saved data; no uninstall is needed to replay onboarding.
  • The redesigned setup, responsive waves, guided desktop demo, and instant Escape/click reveal from Beta 12 remain available.

Drag WieldMac.app into Applications and open it there, or update through Wield’s built-in updater. Wield Dev remains a separate personal test app.

For macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel. Older macOS versions and physical Intel Macs still need runtime testing.
