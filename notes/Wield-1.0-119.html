Wield Beta 15

Version 1.0 (119)

  • Escape in Files, Calculator, Clipboard and Emoji goes back to the prompt on the first press, every time, including after reopening a utility and typing.
  • Steadier on real desktops: a display that drops out briefly on wake or reconnect no longer reshuffles tiled windows, windows return to a display when it comes back, and capture recovers cleanly after sleep, lock and keyboard changes.
  • One quiet control style across Studio, with switches for every preference, a cleaner command editor and a single footer. Utilities no longer clip results or empty-state text.
  • Setup asks about Files and Clipboard explicitly, practises with your real results, offers optional window lessons, and resumes unfinished setup from Learn Wield.
  • Refreshed glass and ink motion in the prompt, utilities and window controls.
  • Anonymous usage counts are content-free and can be turned off in Studio → System → Privacy.

Beta 14 updates automatically. Beta 13 and earlier: quit Wield and replace WieldMac.app with this download; keep saved data.

Universal build for macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel. Runtime verification is on macOS 27 / Apple Silicon; older macOS, physical Intel, physical multi-display, sleep and lid events, and a full visible walkthrough remain pending.
